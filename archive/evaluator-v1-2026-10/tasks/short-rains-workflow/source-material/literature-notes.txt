Saji, Goswami, Vinayachandran and Yamagata (1999), A dipole mode in the tropical
Indian Ocean, Nature 401, 360–363. DOI https://doi.org/10.1038/43854
Primary source: https://www.nature.com/articles/43854 , Abstract and Figures 1–4.
The observational analysis links western Indian Ocean warming and cooling near
Sumatra with coupled rainfall anomalies. It identifies increased rainfall in
eastern Africa during active dipole events and motivates forecasting potential.
This establishes a physical association, not performance of the supplied
antecedent predictor, model forecast index or nested selection procedure.

Climate Hazards Center, CHIRPS v3 data documentation:
https://chc.ucsb.edu/data/chirps3 , product description and Data Citation.
CHIRPS v3 is a satellite/station rainfall product over land at 0.05 degrees from
1981. The final monthly product uses best available station inputs and becomes
available during the following month. Historical final products in a reforecast
study are not historical real-time release vintages. Documentation describes
monthly and pentad totals as the underlying products.
Data DOI https://doi.org/10.15780/G2JQ0P
Scientific description DOI https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-026-07096-4

Frozen acquisition evidence has priority for the supplied numbers' units:

old-normalize.py.txt contains the conversion dividing values by a flat 30.0.
fetch-fixtures.py.txt strips inherited attributes. starting-bench.py.txt later
multiplies calendar month lengths. That last operation is not the inverse of
the frozen acquisition converter.
