Fine source: v4zeek/data/ea_precip_monthly.nc, CHIRPSv3 final monthly records
1981–2023 acquired through legacy rosetta. v4zeek/src/fetch_fixtures.py strips
inherited attrs; legacy src/rosetta/normalize.py divides monthly total by30 and
strips unit evidence. Undoing this documented conversion gives actual monthly
CHIRPS totals; sum March+April+May, never multiply by calendar days again.
Source SHA/coordinates are recorded in source-audit.json. Fine coordinates have
small floating drift: task snaps to declared nominal .05grid, max3.26e-6degrees.
Native cell values are retained; exact spherical areas refer to this nominal grid.
