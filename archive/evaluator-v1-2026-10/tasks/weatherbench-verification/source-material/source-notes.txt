WeatherBench2 source context — development snapshot, 2026-10-05

Rasp et al. (2024), WeatherBench 2: A Benchmark for the Next Generation of
Data-Driven Global Weather Models, doi:10.1029/2023MS004019, provides the paper
anchor. Its public evaluation infrastructure supports reproducible comparisons
on common datasets and explicitly distinguishes forecast verification against
ERA5 from verification against model analysis. This task evaluates one compact
January temperature subset; it does not reproduce full published results.

Code: google-research/weatherbench2, pinned revision
d2c6a1553a0c532332d4c2d3be285508c514bfc2, weatherbench2/metrics.py, Apache-2.0.
The supplied excerpts retain the source license notice. Metric.compute averages
compute_chunk results over time/init_time; MSE computes weighted spatial squared
error. The preferred headline RMSE takes sqrt after temporal MSE aggregation.
ACC computes spatial anomaly covariance over the product of spatial anomaly
norms using verification-time climatology. It does not center anomalies a second
time or pool all times before normalization.

Data guide: https://weatherbench2.readthedocs.io/en/latest/data-guide.html
Official evaluation: https://weatherbench2.readthedocs.io/en/latest/official-evaluation.html
The 64x32 grid is evenly spaced, without pole centers, and was already regridded
with first-order conservative weights. Paper evaluations used another
resolution. The source fields use Kelvin. Forecast coordinates denote
initialization time and prediction timedelta; climatology uses dayofyear/hour.

Source stores and native chunks are hashed in the controller source audit.
ERA5/IFS/climatology bytes are real. Supplied compact arrays are decoded subsets,
not original full global store files; their hashes identify the actual subset.
All values are supplied locally. An agent must not claim a fresh provider
download. Frozen input versions are not pristine held-out weather cases.

The curator introduced separate availability masks and the erroneous comparison
script solely to test diagnosis. Do not attribute those constructions to the
paper authors, ECMWF, Google, or a reported production outage.
