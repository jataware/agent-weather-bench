Pinned sources for regional subseasonal precipitation optimization

Dataset: SubseasonalClimateUSA, Mouatadid et al. (NeurIPS 2023),
https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.10399
Models: Adaptive bias correction for improved subseasonal forecasting,
Mouatadid et al., Nature Communications 14, 3482 (2023),
https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-023-38874-y
Implementation: https://github.com/microsoft/subseasonal_toolkit
Data loader revision: microsoft/subseasonal_data
29307bbd9f511f251ca0f0975bdd9e1b3d68c421
https://github.com/microsoft/subseasonal_data/blob/29307bbd9f511f251ca0f0975bdd9e1b3d68c421/DATA.md

This task uses two public dataframes:
  dataframes/gt-us_precip_1.5x1.5-14d.h5
  dataframes/iri-cfsv2-precip-all-us1_5-ensembled.h5
Azure container: https://subseasonalusa.blob.core.windows.net/subseasonalusa
The official public token service supplies anonymous temporary read permission;
no private account credential was used. Tokens were neither logged nor retained.

Observations are CPC gauge-based precipitation on the source 1.5-degree CONUS
land grid. They represent 14-day precipitation TOTAL in mm from start_date,
not daily average rainfall. Source interpolation used area_hi2lores_Wrap with
cosine-latitude weights and required half the input support.

The CFSv2 source contains daily ensemble-mean two-week forecasts downloaded
from IRI. Its values are already 14-day total mm; do not multiply by14 again.
The processed source averages issuance(t),lead(l) with issuance(t-1),lead(l+1).
The column iri_cfsv2_precip-14.5d is paired in this task with the observed
14-day total beginning issuance_date+14days. Source half-day lead labels
reflect the source daily aggregation convention; task target_start gives the
explicit calendar mapping. Forecasts are not independent member samples.

The compact subset fixes southwest land cells latitude33,34.5,36 and
longitude246,247.5,249 degrees_east, retaining source cell values unchanged.
Weekly Sunday issues are selected. Incomplete issues are removed jointly,
without examining any algorithm's skill; the acquisition audit records this.
Training targets must end before2015-01-01. Development issues begin2015-01-01,
with targets ending before2018-01-01. Final issues cover2018-2021.
Thus training/development target windows do not cross into the next split.

This is a RETROSPECTIVE benchmark snapshot downloaded2026-10-05.
Original release times and real-time data vintages are not reconstructed.
Historical reforecasts/model upgrades and later processing may differ from
what an operational forecaster could have known. Final outcomes are already
public historical records and are not a pristine scientific holdout; controller
withholding still tests the agent's declared experiment boundaries.

This regional task uses pooled cosine-weighted RMSE, with skill1-RMSE/RMSEbaseline.
It is not the global WMO2021 challenge leaderboard, not its RPSS, and does not
claim the published paper's CONUS-wide result. The official archived WMO/ECMWF
challenge endpoints were audited first; old host DNS and migrated object-store
HTTP403 made that route unavailable. This is the named real-data fallback.
