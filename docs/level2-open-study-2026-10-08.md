Existing Level 2 already supports open forecasting research. In this two-attempt pilot, both Luna and Astra chose their own methods, used development-score feedback, and delivered valid reproducible forecasts. Luna beat the existing checked control quickly. This instance does not establish the desired Luna capability boundary. Astra conducted a more elaborate search and obtained a better development score, but generalized less well on the final period.

This uses the open-method, validation-feedback, separate-final-score pattern of [AIRS-Bench](https://github.com/facebookresearch/airs-bench), without installing another framework or changing the task. It does not reproduce AIRS compute budgets or establish comparable difficulty. The tested instance is `weeks34-rainfall`, Level 2, `final-2018-2021--all-cells`, spec v4, fingerprint `453f237dafbfe0d3389eb0009d8cd485ccea6bbbcc672e18b87872ce907ce163`. This is compact subseasonal postprocessing: nine southwest-US cells, already prepared CFSv2 forecasts and rainfall training targets, 147 development issues and 208 final issues. Acquisition is not part of this existing task. The historical final period had already been inspected in earlier investigations; it is an internal research/regression split, not a fresh capability holdout.

Both submitted their best measured candidate, query 5, with exact development-array agreement against the controller's frozen snapshot. No malformed requests or limit denials occurred. Luna chose training-observation means by calendar target month and cell, plus 0.1 times the current raw forecast's anomaly from its corresponding training mean. It did not implement explicit best-candidate checkpointing throughout search; its last candidate happened to be best.

Astra investigated seasonal curves, regularized current and lagged forecast calibration, tree residual models, and causal adaptation to past forecast drift. Its final blend uses 50% seasonal climatology, 12% calibrated CFSv2, 23% tree calibration and 15% adaptive calibration. It reconstructed the constituent error Gram matrix from four development mean squared errors and known pairwise prediction distances, then optimized a continuum of convex blends before scoring the final ensemble. This is valid use of aggregate feedback, not access to private labels. **Five score requests do not mean only five candidate choices.** The development-to-final ranking reversal makes this a useful observation about generalization rather than a reason to prohibit method choice.

Keep Level 2 as the research mechanism. The next difficulty experiment should use a canonical forecast problem with richer predictors or broader geographic coverage and a stronger fixed, independently reproduced comparator. Establish attainable headroom before inference, then freeze fresh verification data. Increasing algorithm scaffolding, forcing an irrelevant helper, or treating a packaging failure as scientific inability would not address what this pilot found.
