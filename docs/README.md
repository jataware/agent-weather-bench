# The documentation starts with the roadmap

Start with [the roadmap](roadmap.md). It summarises the system, what remains to build and the experiments to run, in three pages.

- [The results so far](results.md) give the outcomes of the attempts made to date, the judge's record, and a diagram of how one attempt works.
- [The task set](task-set.md) lists the 25 templates (parameterised tasks), their modes and levels, and what each exists to compare.
- [The assessment format](assessment-format.md) is the specification: the submission envelope, the three modes, the check types, the judge, certification, tolerances and what a result reports.
- [The changelog](CHANGELOG.md) is the dated history of the format and the controller, with the attempts made while building them.
- [Templates](../templates/README.md) explains the `assessment/` package: the template folder layout, the commands, tolerances and certification.
- [The WMO objective seasonal forecasting checklist](../standards/wmo-objective-seasonal-forecasting/README.md) is the first standard for process-mode templates. It is a draft.
- [The brand guide](assets/brand/brand-guide.md) holds the logo, the diagrams and the share images.

The first evaluator (generation 1: ten task packages, a rubric evaluator and a locked judge) and its guides are under [`archive/evaluator-v1-2026-10/`](../archive/evaluator-v1-2026-10/README.md). Superseded documents and the calibration studies of that evaluator are under [`archive/docs-2026-10/`](../archive/docs-2026-10/README.md). The completed pilot is under [`archive/pilot-2026-10-01/`](../archive/README.md).
