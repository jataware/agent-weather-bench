# The centre's objective seasonal forecast practice

This document names the practice. It gives no code and no worked example. Where it names a choice as open, the choice is yours; record it in `answer.json` under `choices`. Where it fixes a convention, the controller's reference uses exactly that convention, and the error bounds on the fixed arrays are tight (parts in a million of the value), so follow the fixed conventions to the letter and keep every computation in float64 from the first read of the files.

## Inputs

- **Models.** Four NMME centres: CCSM4, GEOS-S2S, CanSIPS-IC4 and CFSv2, each as served: the target-season precipitation per member for every initialization year of the training period, on the model's own one-degree grid, in the units each file declares. The ensemble mean is the plain mean over the served members. A model's ensemble-mean seasonal total is carried to the target grid by bilinear interpolation from the served grid points to the target cell centres.
- **Observations.** CHIRPS v2.0 monthly rainfall at 0.05 degrees. Seasonal totals are the sum of the target months. The centre works on the 0.5-degree grid in `target-grid.nc`; the source cells inside a target cell are the hundred whose centres lie within a quarter degree of the target centre in latitude and in longitude, and a target cell is supported when every one of them is observed in every training season. Aggregating the 0.05-degree field to the target grid is open: an area-conservative mean (the mean of the hundred source cells weighted by the cosine of each source cell's centre latitude) and a bilinear interpolation at the cell centre are both the centre's practice; sampling a single source cell is not.
- **Sea-surface temperature.** ERSST v5 monthly SST on its two-degree grid for the training index; the CFSv2 ensemble-mean forecast SST for the target season, from the same initialization, for the forecast-year index. A season mean of monthly SST is the plain mean of the target months' fields. The CFSv2 SST delivery is labelled in kelvin and holds degrees Celsius; standardization makes the unit immaterial.
- **Seasonal totals.** Where a model is served as a seasonal-mean rate, its seasonal total is that rate times the number of days in the season, using the calendar; where it is served as a season total, it is used as such.
- **Unsupported cells.** Every array with latitude and longitude dimensions holds zero on unsupported cells: totals, probabilities, categories, stream counts and scores alike. The support array holds zero there and one elsewhere.

## Categories

- Tercile boundaries per cell are the one-third and two-thirds quantiles of the training-year observed seasonal totals, by linear interpolation of the ordered sample (Hyndman and Fan type 7, NumPy's default `linear` method). An observation within one millionth of a millimetre (1e-6 mm) of a boundary is near normal; the centre adopted this margin because CHIRPS repeats identical seasonal totals across years in dry cells, so boundaries often fall exactly on observed values.
- For any historical year the boundaries, and every fitted quantity, are computed without that year's observation (leave-one-year-out). The forecast year uses all training years.
- Inside a fold, the fitting years are labelled with the fold's own boundaries, computed from those fitting years. The forecast fit labels all training years with the all-years boundaries.

## Stream 1: WVG

- The Western-V Gradient index is the standardized Nino 3.4 mean minus the average of the standardized West North Pacific and West Equatorial Pacific means. The boxes are Nino 3.4: 5S–5N, 190–240E; WNP: 20–35N, 160–210E; WEP: 15S–20N, 120–160E. A grid cell belongs to a box when its centre latitude and longitude lie on or within the box edges. A box mean is the plain mean of the ocean grid cells inside the box; land and missing cells do not count.
- Each box mean is standardized once over the training years, with the mean and the sample standard deviation (divisor n−1) of its training series; the standardization is not repeated per fold.
- Training index: the observed target-season mean SST per training year. Forecast-year index: the CFSv2 ensemble-mean forecast SST for the target season, standardized against CFSv2's own hindcast climatology of the same box, initialization and season over the training years (the mean and sample standard deviation of the ensemble-mean box series over the training years, the forecast year's own issuance excluded).
- Per cell, the probability of below-normal rainfall is a logistic regression of the below-normal indicator on the index, fitted by unpenalized maximum likelihood; any solver that converges to the maximum-likelihood coefficients is acceptable, and the controller allows solver differences of two parts in a hundred thousand in probability. Where the index perfectly separates the below-normal years from the others, the probability is the limiting step function with its step midway between the nearest years of each class. Where every fitting year has the same label, the probability is that label's frequency: zero when no fitting year is below normal, one when all are.
- The centre fixes the near-normal probability at one third and gives the remainder to above normal; when below normal exceeds two thirds the remainder goes to near normal instead.

## Stream 2: eReg

- Per model and cell, observed seasonal rainfall is regressed on the model's ensemble-mean seasonal total (on the target grid) by ordinary least squares with an intercept. The residual variance is the sum of squared residuals of the fitting years divided by n−2, where n is the number of fitting years. The forecast distribution is Gaussian about the fitted value; whether its variance is inflated for the uncertainty of the fitted line (the prediction-error variance, s²(1 + 1/n + (x₀−x̄)²/Σ(x−x̄)²) at the predictor value x₀) or left as the residual variance s² is open.
- Tercile probabilities come from that distribution at the cell's boundaries. The models' probabilities are averaged with equal weight. The reported fitted mean is the equal-weight mean across models of each model's leave-one-year-out fitted value.

## Stream 3: CCA

- Per model, a canonical correlation analysis relates the model's ensemble-mean seasonal rainfall field over the served domain (predictor) to the observed seasonal rainfall on the supported target cells (predictand). Fields are centred, standardized per grid point and weighted by the square root of the cosine of latitude.
- The numbers of predictor EOF modes (one to eight), predictand EOF modes (one to six) and canonical modes (one to three) are chosen per model by cross-validated skill over the fitting years; the selection rule's skill measure is open. For a historical year, the selection is an inner leave-one-year-out over the other training years, so neither the choice of modes nor the fit touches that year's observation; for the forecast, the selection is over all training years, and its canonical mode count is the one reported per model. The centre's CPT configuration applies a gamma transformation to the predictand; applying it is open.
- Forecast uncertainty per cell is a Student-t distribution about the predicted value with the cross-validated residual variance of the fitting years, inflated by (1 + 1/n) for the prediction, and degrees of freedom n minus the predictor modes minus one, where n is the number of fitting years. Tercile probabilities come from that distribution. The models' probabilities are averaged with equal weight. The reported hindcast mean is the equal-weight mean across models of each model's leave-one-year-out predicted rainfall.
- The CCA arrays are not matched to the controller's numbers; they are gated by agreement with the controller's own CCA on the same inputs (pattern correlation of the hindcast probabilities at least 0.6, cross-validated RPSS within 0.08 of the controller's).

## Combination, verification and issue

- The objective forecast is the equal-weight average of the three streams' probabilities at every supported cell. The stream count array holds three on supported cells and zero elsewhere.
- Verification uses the ranked probability score of the leave-one-year-out hindcasts against the observed categories, per stream and for the objective, and the skill score against the equal-thirds climatology, averaged without weights over supported cells.
- The issued outlook is the objective forecast for the forecast year; the dominant category is the most probable one, with exact ties to near normal.
