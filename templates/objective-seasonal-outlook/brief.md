# Issue the objective seasonal rainfall outlook

Produce the regional centre's objective {season_name} rainfall outlook for {forecast_year}, issued in {init_month_name}, for the Greater Horn of Africa window defined in `target-grid.nc`, and write the bulletin. Follow the centre's three-stream practice described in `practice.md`: a logistic regression on the Western-V-Gradient sea-surface-temperature index (WVG), a per-cell ensemble regression on each model's ensemble mean (eReg), and a canonical correlation analysis of model rainfall against observed rainfall (CCA), combined with equal weight.

You have a source service, not prepared arrays. Read `acquisition.json` in the input directory, retrieve its catalogue over HTTP, inspect `provenance.json`, and download what you need: NMME seasonal precipitation for the four centres, CFSv2 forecast sea-surface temperature, CHIRPS monthly rainfall at 0.05 degrees, and ERSST v5 monthly sea-surface temperature. The service is a frozen snapshot of what acmadDL delivered; it is not a live provider. Observations for {forecast_year} are withheld. Retrieve the data again when the controller runs your command on a fresh snapshot; submission caches must not override it.

The training years are {training_start}–{training_end} without {forecast_year}. Every historical forecast must be made as if {forecast_year} and the year being forecast were unknown: a year's own observation must not enter its own hindcast at any stage. The forecast for {forecast_year} uses all training years and that year's model fields alone.

Deliver `results.zarr` with coordinates `year` (the training years), `model` = 0,1,2,3 for CCSM4, GEOS-S2S, CanSIPS-IC4, CFSv2 in that order, `category` = 0,1,2 for below, near, above normal, `stream` = 0,1,2 for CCA, eReg, WVG, `lat` and `lon` exactly as in `target-grid.nc`, and `plat`, `plon` the model grid as served. Totals are in mm per season; probabilities lie in [0, 1] and sum to one on supported cells; unsupported cells hold zero in every probability and total array and zero in the support array. No array may contain missing values. Dimension order is free.

Stage arrays, each named with its stage prefix:

- `prepare_observed_mm(year,lat,lon)`, `prepare_support(lat,lon)` (1 where every 0.05-degree CHIRPS cell inside the target cell is finite in every training season, else 0), `prepare_model_hindcast_mean_mm(model,year,lat,lon)`, `prepare_model_forecast_mean_mm(model,lat,lon)`, `prepare_model_predictor_mm(model,year,plat,plon)` and `prepare_model_predictor_forecast_mm(model,plat,plon)` (ensemble-mean seasonal totals on the served model grid), `prepare_tercile_lower_mm(lat,lon)`, `prepare_tercile_upper_mm(lat,lon)` (from all training years), `prepare_observed_category(year,lat,lon)` (each year classified with boundaries that exclude that year; a total within 1e-6 mm of a boundary is near normal), `prepare_sst_index(year)`, scalar `prepare_sst_index_forecast`.
- `wvg_hindcast_probability(year,category,lat,lon)`, `wvg_forecast_probability(category,lat,lon)`.
- `ereg_model_hindcast_probability(model,year,category,lat,lon)`, `ereg_model_forecast_probability(model,category,lat,lon)`, `ereg_hindcast_probability(year,category,lat,lon)`, `ereg_forecast_probability(category,lat,lon)`, `ereg_fitted_mean_mm(year,lat,lon)` (the equal-weight mean across models of each model's cross-validated fitted rainfall).
- `cca_hindcast_probability(year,category,lat,lon)`, `cca_forecast_probability(category,lat,lon)`, `cca_hindcast_mean_mm(year,lat,lon)`, `cca_modes_selected(model)` (the number of canonical modes retained per model).
- `combine_hindcast_probability(year,category,lat,lon)`, `combine_forecast_probability(category,lat,lon)`, `combine_available_streams(lat,lon)`.
- `verify_stream_rps(stream,lat,lon)`, `verify_stream_rpss(stream)`, `verify_objective_rps(lat,lon)`, `verify_climatology_rps(lat,lon)`, scalar `verify_objective_rpss`. RPS is the sum over the three ordered categories of squared differences between cumulative forecast and cumulative observed probability, averaged over the training years per cell; RPSS is one minus the ratio of the cell-mean RPS to that of the equal-thirds climatology, both over supported cells.
- `issue_forecast_probability(category,lat,lon)`, identical to the combined forecast, and `issue_dominant_category(lat,lon)` (ties go to near normal).

In `answer.json`, give the rerunnable command and these claims as lists of `positive` or `nonpositive`: `hindcast_skill` with four entries, the sign of the cross-validated RPSS of CCA, eReg, WVG and the objective in that order. Record in `choices` the readings you adopted where the practice leaves a choice.

In `report.md`, write the bulletin: the outlook for {season_name} {forecast_year}, its historical skill, the data and their lineage, how each stream was built, and the uncertainty in language a non-specialist could act on. Every number the bulletin states must appear in the submitted arrays. The report's scientific content is reviewed separately from the numbers. Withheld-season skill is scored by the controller and does not decide completion.
