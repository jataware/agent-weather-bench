Data notes for the weeks 3-4 rainfall task

Source: SubseasonalClimateUSA (Mouatadid et al., 2023, https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.10399).
Observations are CPC gauge-based precipitation on a 1.5-degree grid.
Forecasts are CFSv2 ensemble-mean reforecasts distributed by IRI.

Variables
  precipitation  observed 14-day rainfall TOTAL in mm, beginning at target_start
  raw_cfsv2      the model's forecast of that same 14-day total, in mm
Both are already 14-day totals. Neither is a daily rate.

Coordinates
  issue_time     the Sunday on which the forecast was issued
  target_start   issue_time + 14 days; the target window is the 14 days from target_start
  location       grid-cell index; latitude and longitude (degrees east) give its centre

Splits
  training.nc holds every issue whose target window closed before the training boundary.
  The two feature files hold later issues and carry no observations.
  Target windows never cross from one split into the next.

Scoring
  RMSE = sqrt( sum over issues and cells of cos(latitude) * error^2
               / (number of issues * sum over cells of cos(latitude)) )
  Skill against a baseline is 1 - RMSE / RMSE_baseline. The two baselines are the
  raw model forecast and a seasonal climatology fitted to the training observations.

Limits
  This is a retrospective snapshot downloaded on 2026-10-05. Original release times
  are not reconstructed. The withheld observations are public historical records.
  Consecutive weekly 14-day windows overlap, so cases are not independent.
